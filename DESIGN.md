# NetraDrift visual and product context

## Intent
Public interactive MPLADS demonstration with 36 fully synthetic works, fictional representatives and illustrative infrastructure. A separately authenticated CSV research workspace retains source-backed analysis. The demo personas never grant real public-office authority.

## Visual system and token mapping
The user's sixteen reference images are the layout authority: white and warm orange, a bold compact three-column landing, connected MP nodes, restrained district map, a five-step peer investigation and detailed timelines. `app/demo.css` owns `--nd-accent: #bf6848`, `--nd-ink: #29352d`, `--nd-line: #e3e5dc`, `--nd-paper: #f8f9f5`, `--nd-sage: #7d9673`. Existing research tokens remain in `app/globals.css`. Demo body text 14px+, secondary text 12px+, main controls 13px+. Sans-serif UI, Georgia investigation headlines, condensed bold landing headline. White bordered 10px panels, modest shadows only around the network. User requested reference direction overrides generic visual defaults.

## Composition and motion
The landing connects a headline/stat rail, dominant projected 3D network/map, and filter rail. Work selection continues into peer comparison, details, timeline and evidence. Animated connections, drag/keyboard rotation, zoom, orbiting peer search and progressively drawn benchmarks express real relations. Global pause and reduced-motion support. Mobile stacks panels; tables alone own horizontal scrolling. Vector portraits and infrastructure are clearly illustrative, never submitted proof.

## Domain boundaries
Rules compute unit-cost ratios, overdue deadlines and synthetic duplicate pairs. No fraud probabilities. Peer sets exclude the selected work and require category/terrain/year and comparable quantity. Camera/GPS are user-initiated; reports await review and do not mutate baseline progress. Action plans are deterministic, evidence-grounded recommendations. Method search retrieves maintained explanations; no external RAG language model is configured.
